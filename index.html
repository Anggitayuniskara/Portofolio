About

My name is A. A. Anggita Yuniskara Dewi, you can call me Anggi. I'm a Geography graduate from Universitas Indonesia. During my time as a student, I grew interested in working with data, recognizing patterns, and understanding how different elements relate to one another. I became increasingly interested in how data and patterns can tell meaningful stories. This portfolio showcases selected projects and experiences that reflect my interest in understanding spatial patterns and using geospatial data to explore real-world problems.
