About

My name is A. A. Anggita Yuniskara Dewi, you can call me Anggi. I'm a Geography graduate from Universitas Indonesia. During my time as a student, I grew interested in working with data, recognizing patterns, and understanding how different elements relate to one another. I became increasingly interested in how data and patterns can tell meaningful stories. This portfolio showcases selected projects and experiences that reflect my interest in understanding spatial patterns and using geospatial data to explore real-world problems.

Skills

Hard Skills

Spatial analysis and GIS mapping, remote sensing and satellite image classification, cartography and map design, spatial database management (PostGIS), flood and hydrological modeling, multi-criteria land suitability analysis, geospatial programming (Python, Google Earth Engine), machine learning for spatial prediction, field and bathymetric surveys, environmental impact and ESG analysis, data visualization and dashboards.

Soft Skills

Analytical and critical thinking, attention to detail, problem solving, adaptability, clear communication of technical results, empathy and interpersonal skills, teamwork and collaboration, time management and organization, leadership and initiative, conflict resolution, emotional intelligence, continuous learning.
